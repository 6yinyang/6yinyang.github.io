i was sitting in the dining hall today after aurafarming in my sleep, like i usually do.\
since it was after 2:30, the actual food was all gone so there's only the carbohydrates and salad left.\
so i got 2 slices of pepperoni pizza. the cheese on the pepperoni pizza looked stretchy and appetizing.\
i also got a salad so i can healthfarm and not die. [#bryanjohnson](https://www.youtube.com/BryanJohnson)

so i sat down. and looked at my pizza.\
the plate was dirty from the struggle to separate the slices and get them onto my plate.\
there was a streaky mix of oil and ash and cheese. the more i look at it, the more abominable it becomes.\
it looks like someone had desecrated my plate of man-made sustenance.

at that point i had already eaten a slice. i could not bring myself to eat another.

pepperoni pizza is an amalgamation beyond my comprehension.\
bread that's not really bread.\
cheese that's not really cheese.\
tomato that's not really tomato.\
meat that's not really meat.\
warmth that's not really warmth.

i put the plate down.\
and ate my salad.\
mmm, i feel nature coming into my mouth.\
some glimmer of relief it is.
